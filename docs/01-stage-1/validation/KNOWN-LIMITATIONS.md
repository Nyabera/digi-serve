## 2. Synthetic Stage 1 product limitations

- one fully functional Transcript Request service; two simplified demos;
- one fixed Records → Finance handoff, no transfer/arbitrary sharing/workflow engine;
- seeded form/requirements/workflow with limited safe metadata editing, no builders;
- in-app notifications only; external email required before pilot;
- elapsed-clock SLA only, no business hours/holidays/pause/escalation ladders;
- basic real operational aggregates only; no custom reports, rankings or predictive analytics;
- no malware scanning, OCR, advanced signatures or public verification;
- no automatic version migration; requests remain pinned;
- no general organization/role switcher and no Platform Admin organization workspace;
- no ordinary hard-delete/purge path until retention/privacy policy is approved;
- global search, advanced admin surfaces and standalone audit/report pages are not Stage 1 dependencies.
