# FAIDIA Stage 1 — Limited External Pilot Plan

**Status:** NOT_AUTHORIZED_READY_FOR_REVIEW  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-14  
**Authority:** Stage 0 DEC-026–028 and external-pilot validation list

## 1. Pilot thesis

The first approved milestone is a synthetic demonstration. A real-data pilot is a later, limited, supported validation exercise—not an automatic consequence of completing software.

## 2. Entry criteria

All Documentation/Implementation gates pass; staging vertical slice accepted; production security/privacy/retention/backup/monitoring/incident checks approved; institutional owner validates current process, eligibility, fee/reference, Finance output, Registrar authority, final outcome, closure, SLA and support; email verification/recovery/delivery is operational; participant consent/notice and data-processing authority are documented.

## 3. Proposed cohort and duration

Use one institution, Transcript Request only, the three approved departments/profiles, a small controlled applicant cohort and named supported staff for a time-boxed four-week pilot. Exact participant count, dates and service volume must be inserted by a separate approved pilot authorization; they are intentionally not fabricated here.

## 4. Preparation

- train staff by role using synthetic cases;
- verify least-privilege accounts and support/escalation contacts;
- baseline current lost-request/status-enquiry/processing observations;
- test backup restore, incident response and rollback/stop controls;
- publish applicant guidance, privacy/support information and known limitations;
- confirm daily operational review and weekly feedback owners.

## 5. Monitoring and stop criteria

Daily monitor failed jobs, access denials, overdue work, outcome failures, support load and data-quality issues. Immediately pause new submissions for suspected cross-tenant/ownership/file exposure, material data loss/corruption, incorrect outcome issuance, unavailable recovery/backup, or institutional authority withdrawal. Preserve evidence and follow incident procedure.

## 6. Exit criteria

Complete planned cases or authorized early stop; reconcile every request; export no sensitive ad-hoc dataset; close/respond to high-severity issues; produce validation report and recommendation: proceed, extend with conditions, revise/retest or stop. Pilot completion never auto-promotes postponed scope.
